01 — About

A student of both software and systems.

I care about the layer underneath the model — how data is shaped, how a system scales, and how a hackathon idea becomes a working prototype in 36 hours.

I'm currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science at the Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST), where I've built everything from embedded assistive devices to CPU schedulers. That range — hardware to algorithms to applied AI — is deliberate. I want to understand a system end to end before I trust a model built on top of it.

Outside coursework, I train in applied AI (computer vision, NLP, LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation), practice competitive programming, and most recently, I've been pulled toward quantum computing — winning the Alexandria Quantum Hackathon 2026 was the moment that turned curiosity into a direction.
